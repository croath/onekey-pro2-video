Alternatives: `OneKey Pro 2 | Hardware Wallet` · `Keep Your Keys at Home — OneKey Pro 2`

**Description**

Keep your keys at home. Only the signatures go.

OneKey Pro 2 is a hardware wallet as thin as a card: glass on the front and the back, a metal frame, and a screen with almost no border. Transactions go in and out as QR codes through the rear camera, so it never needs a cable or a connection. Four secure elements keep your keys, SignGuard stops you before you "approve all", and every transaction is shown in plain words before you sign. Fingerprint unlock, FIDO and passkeys, and open-source firmware you can read line by line.
